# Block Me If You Can - Mobile_Game

Welcome to **Block Me If You Can**, a fun and challenging mobile game built in Unity 3D, inspired by the traditional Filipino street game **Patintero**! In this fast-paced arcade game, your objective is to make it past opponents and avoid getting tagged with time pressure. With each level, the challenge intensifies, making it perfect for those who love action-packed gameplay with a cultural twist.

---

### Gameplay
In **Block Me If You Can**, your objective is to make it through different quadrants, avoiding getting tagged by opponents. Each quadrant adds a time-pressured challenge, forcing you to move swiftly and strategically, or risk getting trapped!

#### Time Pressure Mechanic:
As you move through each quadrant, you have **3 seconds** to reach the next one. If you fail to reach the new quadrant within this time frame, you lose, and the game resets. This adds a layer of intensity and urgency, requiring quick thinking and fast reflexes to avoid opponents and reach safety.

#### Game Inspired by Patintero:
In **Patintero**, players must cross a series of lines while avoiding getting tagged by defenders. In **Block Me If You Can**, this concept is translated into a digital experience where you must navigate through defenders (obstacles) without being tagged, while also managing the time pressure.

---

### Controls
- Directional Pad: Move your player to avoid being tagged by opponents.
- Bump into Power-ups: Use special power-ups like freezing enemies or boosting your stamina.

---

### Power-Ups
Power-ups can give you the edge you need to survive against tougher enemies and make it through time-pressured quadrants.

**Available Power-Ups:**
- **Freeze:** Freezes all enemies for a limited time, allowing you to safely pass through the quadrant.
- **Stamina Boost:** Provides additional stamina to your player, helping you dash faster and avoid opponents in critical moments.

---

### Development
This game was developed using Unity 3D, a cross-platform game engine, and built specifically for Android devices.

**Technologies Used:**

- **Unity 3D 2020.1.11f1:** Game development engine for creating 3D environments and gameplay mechanics.
- **C#:** Scripting language used for gameplay logic, AI, and controls.
- **Android SDK:** For building and deploying the game on Android devices.
- **Blender:** 3D modeling tool for character, environment and UI design.
- **Mixamo:** Publicly available mocap (motion capture) animations
